Privacy Policy

Gambit Go: Offline Chess  ·  com.technoorstudio.gambitgo

Effective date: September 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Technoor Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application Gambit Go: Offline Chess (the "App"). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

The short version: Gambit Go is an offline chess game. We do not require an account, we do not ask for your name or email to play, and your game data and settings stay on your device. We do not operate any game servers and we do not collect personal information on our own servers. The Android version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device and advertising information as described below.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number in order to play. We only receive personal information from you if you choose to contact us directly (for example, by emailing our support address), in which case we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

Information stored on your device

Your game preferences and progress — such as selected board theme, piece set, sound and haptic settings, unlocked cosmetics, and onboarding status — are stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed if you uninstall the App.

Information collected automatically by third parties

The Android version of the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. These include banner ads, full-screen interstitial ads, native ads shown inside certain dialogs, and rewarded ads used to unlock optional cosmetic items. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect certain information, which may include:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, and similar technical data)
  • Approximate, non-precise location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, and general usage related to advertising)

We do not control the data collected by Google AdMob. This collection is governed by Google's own privacy policies, linked in the "Third-Party Services" section below.

4. Device Permissions

On Android, the App requests only the following permissions, used solely for the purposes described:

PermissionWhy it is used

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, or files.

5. Advertising Choices

You can limit ad personalization at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.

Google may also present a consent prompt for personalized advertising in regions where it is required (for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland). Where such a prompt is shown, your choice is handled by Google's advertising services, and you can revise it at any time through your device's Google Ads settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience chess game and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention

Settings and game data are stored on your device and remain until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Any email correspondence you send us is retained only as long as needed to address your request. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Because we do not maintain user accounts or store your personal information on our servers, most of your data can be cleared simply by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. For requests relating to advertising data, please also refer to Google's privacy resources linked above. You may contact us using the details below for any privacy-related request.

9. Security
